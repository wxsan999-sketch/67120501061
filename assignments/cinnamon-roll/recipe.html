Cinnamon Roll Wreath with Apples

A festive, braided cinnamon roll wreath filled with cinnamon-sugar butter and thin apple slices, finished with a sweet cream cheese glaze. Perfect for breakfast on a holiday morning!

Ingredients you'll need

  • Crescent roll dough – canned crescent roll dough or sheets of canned crescent dough both work. Sheet dough is a little easier since you don't need to press perforations together.
  • Granulated sugar
  • Brown sugar
  • Ground cinnamon
  • Unsalted butter – soften the butter and cream cheese for at least 30 minutes at room temperature before starting.
  • Pazazz apples – sweet, tart and extra crispy. Any firm baking apple works.
  • Cream cheese
  • Milk
  • Vanilla extract
  • Powdered sugar

Step by step instructions

Tip: click a step to mark it as done.

  1. Roll out the crescent roll dough. Place a large piece of parchment paper on a flat work surface. Place both sheets of crescent roll dough slightly overlapping on the parchment paper and press the seams together to form one large rectangle.
  2. Top with the cinnamon sugar butter. In a medium bowl, combine softened butter with granulated sugar, brown sugar and ground cinnamon. Spread this mixture evenly across the crescent roll dough.
  3. Add the apples. Core and thinly slice one Pazazz apple, then place the slices on top of the cinnamon sugar butter in an even layer, not overlapping.
  4. Roll it up. Roll the crescent roll dough up tightly over the apples and the cinnamon sugar butter into a tube.
  5. Slice it in half. Use a sharp knife to slice lengthwise through the middle of the cinnamon roll tube.
  6. Separate the two halves. Make sure the cinnamon butter and apples are facing up on both halves.
  7. Braid them together. Carefully braid the two halves together, lifting one half over the other, then vice versa, until it's one long braid.
  8. Form the wreath. Bring the two ends of the braid together, transfer to a baking sheet, then bake at 350°F for 20–25 minutes until golden brown.

Enjoy!

Drizzle with cream cheese glaze made from cream cheese, milk, vanilla extract and powdered sugar, then slice and serve.
